Our History

Where Tradition Meets Passion

Founded in 2025 in the heart of Rajahmundry, Raajanna Military Hotel was born from a deep love for authentic Andhra military hotel style culinary traditions. We wanted to create a place that served not just food, but nostalgic experiences.

Our specialty lies in fiery, spicy, and hearty meals created strictly using family recipes passed down through generations. The secret is simple: slow-cooked perfection. We believe that good food cannot be rushed. Our chefs spend hours grinding spices by hand and carefully simmering broths to ensure every bite delivers a punch of robust flavors.

Whether you're craving an incredibly fragrant biryani or a deeply spiced chicken fry, our kitchen prepares every dish with unparalleled dedication. We don't just cook for you; we treat you like family.
